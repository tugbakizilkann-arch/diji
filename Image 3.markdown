## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an ultra-modern, high-end editorial aesthetic tailored for a next-generation creative AI studio. It merges the deliberate serenity of architectural minimalism with the precision of computational intelligence. Rather than leaning on typical neon, cyber-inspired AI tropes, the identity communicates authority, bespoke craft, and calm technological mastery.

The visual direction draws from high-luxury editorial publishing and spatial design. Generous negative space, whisper-quiet micro-interactions, warm natural tones, and subtle frosted overlays evoke permanence and discretion. The emotional impact should balance quiet confidence with progressive capability—making clients feel they have stepped into an exclusive digital atelier rather than a tech utility.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in organic, deep forest and olive greens set against warm, tactile off-white surfaces:

- **Canvas & Backdrops:** Base surfaces rely on `#F9F8F5` (warm cream) with subtle sub-layers in `#F4F2EC`. Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved strictly for elevated structural cards, interactive sheets, and focused containers to maintain deliberate contrast.
- **Primary & Accent Greens:** Deep Olive (`#283C2E`) serves as the commanding primary accent for key actions, brand marks, and active states. Mid Olive (`#3D5A45`) handles interactive states and primary boundaries, while Soft Sage (`#5C7B64` and `#8FA695`) acts as an understated accent for subtle highlights, badges, and progress indicators.
- **Typography & Structure:** Primary body copy and titles utilize Deep Slate (`#1C211E`), preventing the visual harshness of pitch black while maintaining WCAG AAA legibility. Secondary metadata and labels use muted slate-tinted tones (`#515A54` and `#7E8881`).
- **Dividers & Strokes:** Borders rely on translucent warm neutrals (`rgba(28, 33, 30, 0.08)` down to `rgba(28, 33, 30, 0.04)`), providing crisp architectural boundaries without visual noise.

## Typography

The typographical voice rests upon an intentional tension between **Newsreader** (an authoritative, modern literary serif with editorial pedigree) and **Plus Jakarta Sans** (a precise, warm, contemporary geometric sans-serif).

- Use `Newsreader` predominantly in italic or regular weights for hero displays, narrative intros, and philosophical statements to anchor the atelier feel.
- Maintain wide line-heights on all body copy (`1.625` to `1.875`) to encourage unhurried reading and convey structural lightness.
- Use uppercase styling strictly for `label-sm` elements (tags, badges, eyebrow indicators), combined with tracked spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.06em`), never for body copy or headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a fixed-max-width 12-column grid capped at `1440px`, flanked by generous fluid outer margins. 

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with `2.5rem` gutters and outer margins scaling up to `5rem`. Section vertical padding uses `space-2xl` to `space-3xl` to preserve editorial breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins.

Content alignment should prioritize asymmetrical rhythm—such as offset column spans (e.g., 5-column editorial text beside a 7-column media frame) rather than uniform, boxy layouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated as architectural and tactile rather than virtual or heavily drop-shadowed. The system uses a disciplined three-tiered surface strategy:

1. **Base Layer:** The warm creamy canvas (`#F9F8F5`).
2. **Elevated Containers & Cards:** Semi-opaque white cards (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) placed directly onto the canvas, supported by fine 1px hairline borders (`rgba(28, 33, 30, 0.06)`).
3. **Floating Overlays & Glass:** Backdrop-filtered surfaces using `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with background tints of `rgba(249, 248, 245, 0.75)`. Used exclusively for sticky headers, navigation pills, and interactive modals.
4. **Shadows:** Minimal and diffused. Shadows do not simulate direct light sources; they simulate ambient soft bounce lighting using a faint olive-slate tint:
   - *Soft Rest:* `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 33, 30, 0.03), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 33, 30, 0.04)`
   - *Hover Lift:* `0 12px 32px -6px rgba(40, 60, 46, 0.08)`

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is organic and approachable yet tailored:

- **Standard Cards & Modules:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) creates a structured, architectural framing that remains friendly.
- **Feature Showcase & Hero Panels:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) for prominent media containers and central dashboard cards.
- **Interactive Pills:** Full capsule geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`) is applied to call-to-action buttons, filter chips, category markers, and status tags, providing an organic contrast against angular grid lines.
- **Form Controls:** Set to `0.75rem` (`12px`) to maintain clean alignment alongside typography.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pill-shaped, Deep Olive background (`#283C2E`), off-white typography (`#F9F8F5`). On hover, smoothly transitions to Mid Olive (`#3D5A45`) with a gentle `translate-y-[-1px]` offset.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pill-shaped, transparent background, thin boundary stroke (`#283C2E`), and text in `#283C2E`. Hover applies a light wash (`rgba(40, 60, 46, 0.05)`).
- **Ghost:** Pill-shaped, no boundary, deep slate text (`#1C211E`). Hover applies soft background tint (`#EFECE6`).

### Cards & Case Modules
- Elevated on pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or frosted white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
- Framed by a 1px border (`rgba(28, 33, 30, 0.06)`).
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).
- Hover states feature a subtle border illumination using `#8FA695` at `30%` opacity and an effortless ambient shadow shift.

### Chips & Tags
- Pill-shaped badges with compact padding (`0.25rem 0.75rem`).
- Muted Olive Tint (`rgba(61, 90, 69, 0.08)`) with Mid Olive text (`#283C2E`) and uppercase tracking (`label-sm`).
- Filter chips include a 1px matching outline that fills to deep olive on selection.

### Form Inputs
- Background in subtle off-white (`#F4F2EC` or `#FFFFFF`).
- 1px border (`rgba(28, 33, 30, 0.1)`).
- Padding: `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus state eliminates standard blue rings, replacing them with a crisp Olive (`#3D5A45`) border and a soft ambient focus ring (`rgba(61, 90, 69, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Rounded squares (`4px` radius) for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Base border `rgba(28, 33, 30, 0.2)`.
- Checked state transitions immediately to `#283C2E` background with a pure `#F9F8F5` checkmark or center dot.

### Editorial Case Study Carousels & AI Metrics
- Split layouts featuring rich media on one side, paired with high-contrast serif quotes and sans-serif metadata tables on the other.
- Data readouts pair large Newsreader numbers with delicate, tracked Plus Jakarta Sans labels.
